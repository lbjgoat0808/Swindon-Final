Our Story

Swindon Book Company was established in 1918, making it one of Hong Kong's oldest and most beloved bookstores. For over a century, we've been at the heart of Hong Kong's literary community, serving generations of readers, students, and book lovers across the city.

"More than a bookstore, we're a cultural landmark and gathering place for Hong Kong's literary community."

Our flagship store in Central has been a cornerstone of the community, known for its warm atmosphere, knowledgeable staff, and carefully curated selection. We focus on English-language books spanning all genres, from classic literature to contemporary bestsellers, academic texts to children's favorites.

What We Do

Swindon Book Company has been the primary textbook supplier for countless schools and universities throughout Hong Kong for decades. But our mission extends far beyond textbooks.

We carry a diverse selection of books and magazines covering lifestyle, art, reference, fiction, and non-fiction. Our collection includes beautiful stationery and cards, making us a one-stop destination for book lovers and gift seekers alike.

Our Heritage

Throughout our history, we've maintained a utilitarian, no-frills approach that places books front and center. While we may not have the flashiest decor, our classic charm and stone facade have made us a recognizable landmark in Hong Kong.

Our Promise

At Swindon Book Company, we believe in personal service. Our friendly, knowledgeable staff are always ready to help you find exactly what you're looking for—or discover something new you didn't know you needed.

If we don't have the book you're searching for, we're more than happy to place a special order for you. Your satisfaction and love of reading are at the heart of everything we do.

Whether you're a longtime customer or visiting us for the first time, we invite you to experience the warmth and character that have made Swindon Book Company a Hong Kong institution for over a century.
